# Wayfinder map: Low-poly 3D pivot

Label: wayfinder:map

Charted 2026-08-18. Successor effort to [`isometric-dashboard`](../isometric-dashboard/map.md), which closed with a build-ready 2D pixel-art spec.

## Destination

A locked decision, plus the spec and ADR updates it forces: **the app renders low-poly 3D Worlds instead of 2D pixel-art sprites, across all three Themes** (Forest, City, RTS). The map is done when the render stack, the asset source, the Size Tier and Signal vocabulary in 3D, and the per-Theme mapping are all decided, and [`spec.md`](../isometric-dashboard/spec.md) §1/§4/§7 plus the render-stack ADR say so. Building the app is a separate effort after this map closes.

The pivot was **gated**: [Prototype: the 3D Forest World (gate)](issues/08-prototype-3d-forest-gate.md) had to earn the same "this lands" verdict the pixel-art Forest got on 2026-08-18. **It passed on 2026-08-24 — "it lands 100× better than the pixel world"** — so the map runs to its destination and PixiJS is retired.

The pixel World lives on only in git history (commit `ed8b8a9`). Two render modes shipping side by side stays ruled out, and there is no longer a 2D path to fall back to.

The plan choice is [ticket 07](issues/07-grilling-free-vs-pro.md), not a budget grep. ~~The free/Pro split is architectural: Pro serves the `.mjs` program so knobs evaluate at runtime; free forces a fixed set of pre-baked variants.~~ **Corrected 2026-08-19 by [research 01](research/01-lowpoly-sources.md):** the `.mjs` module is free for free assets. The real split is **"Forest at runtime, free" versus "City and RTS at all, paid"** — Polyfork's free tier holds 231 props and 9 buildings, and zero characters, vehicles or terrain.
- **Authoring is a third option, live since 2026-08-24** ([ticket 09](issues/09-grilling-city-theme-3d.md)). A Theme may generate its geometry in code instead of buying it, and the City does. Decide it **per Theme**: the Forest stays on Kenney CC0, and one Theme's choice is not a precedent for the next. Measure a candidate kit's triangles per Entity before trusting it — that is what caught Kenney's City Kit at 13.3× the Forest's.
- **CC0 is the baseline; Polyfork is the upgrade** ([research 01](research/01-lowpoly-sources.md)). Kenney and Quaternius cover all three Themes under CC0 at zero cost, zero licence risk and zero vendor dependency. Every ticket should price Polyfork against that floor, not against nothing.
- **Nothing is rigged, in any Theme** ([ticket 16](issues/16-grilling-entity-animation.md), 2026-08-24). Motion lives inside the instance write. **Ambient motion** carries no Signal and is optional; **transition motion** is a tweened geometry swap and runs everywhere. A Theme's moving figures are **Scenery** — a fixed, Signal-blind cast, ~10 draw calls, never scaled by the Subscriber count. Do not re-open this per Theme.
- Domain language lives in [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md). **World**, **Entity**, **Size Tier**, **Signal**, **Theme** and now **Scenery** keep their meanings in 3D — only how an Entity renders changes. The word "pixel-art" in the spec's vision line is prose, not a glossary term; ticket 11 rewrites it.
- Skills to consult per ticket type: `mattpocock-skills:prototype` for prototype tickets; `mattpocock-skills:grilling` + `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling` for grilling tickets; `frontend-design` for visual work. **The repo became git on 2026-08-24** (`rob-vb/dashboard-visualizer`, private, branch `main`) — earlier tickets on this map were written under "no git" and may still say so. The local-markdown tracker in `.scratch/` stays canonical, and research findings go in `.scratch/lowpoly-3d-pivot/research/`.

## Decisions so far

- [Grilling: does an Entity move?](issues/16-grilling-entity-animation.md) — **no skeleton, anywhere, ever; all motion lives inside the instance write, and it splits into two kinds with opposite rules.** The ticket's own premise was wrong and correcting it dissolved the hard question: research 01 never said Ultimate Fantasy RTS ships *rigged units* — the pack is **128 static meshes**, "no parameters, same limitation as Kenney", and the rigs live in *other* Quaternius packs whose styles the same research warns do not match. Nothing rigged was ever chosen. **An RTS unit is Scenery, not an Entity**: the building is the Entity and the pack's evolution stages are its Size Tier ladder, so a soldier represents nobody, and Scenery is a **fixed cast** that costs the same ten draw calls at 237 Entities as at 3,167 — the budget question disappears rather than being paid. The split: **ambient motion** (sway, the City twinkle, any Scenery loop) carries **no Signal ever** and may be switched off with nothing lost; **transition motion** plays once at a state change, means that change, and runs on every device. The code had this split before the map did — `world3d.js:547` gates sway, `world3d.js:541` never gates the tier tween. **Transition motion has exactly two members and one mechanism — a geometry swap, tweened**: a Size Tier change and the day-90 residue swap. My proposal that a churn should play a fall was **withdrawn against [ticket 06](issues/06-grilling-signal-vocabulary-3d.md) Q7**, which is better: the Entity keeps its geometry and stands there tinting for 90 days, because a felled pine says *logging*, not death. Payments get no transition (a World that twitches on every payment reads as noise). Generalised from ticket 05: **transition motion plays live and in replay, hard-cuts on a scrub.** Ambient motion stays desktop-only and off under `prefers-reduced-motion`, but **the recorded reason changes to battery and the demand loop, never performance** — otherwise it reads as exactly the device-sniffing ladder [ticket 15](issues/15-task-measure-device-fps.md) banned; noted honestly that ticket 15's iPhone never ran a continuously animating World, since `world3d.js:173` gates sway off on `pointer: coarse`, so sustained drain is the unmeasured quantity, not peak frame time. Ticket 06's **stopped sway on a dead tree is demoted to redundancy** — it may repeat what colour says and may never be the only cue, or a mobile World of living trees reads as dead. **Scenery is Signal-blind and fixed**: no extra villagers when MRR grows, which would make it semantic against ADR-0001. `CONTEXT.md` gained a **Scenery** entry in-session. Unblocks [ticket 10](issues/10-grilling-rts-theme-3d.md) with a rule and a ~10-draw-call budget; [ticket 11](issues/11-task-update-spec-and-adr.md) gains a **third ADR**.

- [Task: measure real frame rates on a laptop and a phone](issues/15-task-measure-device-fps.md) — **no mobile ceiling: 60 fps on an iPhone 16 at 3,167 Entities with shadows on** (user, 2026-08-24, both worst cases — `scale3000` idle and `scale1583` replay-while-dragging). The map has measured a real frame rate at last, and **ticket 08's one debt is paid**: the pivot is now certified on the look *and* on the hardware it was doubted on. The open risk was never the CPU — 41 draw calls flat, ~3.3 ms per replay frame, the fold fixed by [ticket 12](issues/12-task-fix-fold-performance.md) — it was **268k–516k triangles finding a mobile vertex limit**. It did not. Two honest limits on the reading: **60 is the panel's cap** (iPhone 16 non-Pro is 60 Hz), so the result means *"inside the 16.7 ms budget"* and the headroom stays unquantified — read `frame ms (write / gl)`, never `fps`, for a margin; and **Android, low-end phones and thermal throttling were not tested**, which belongs to the build effort against a real app, not to a prototype on an artifact URL. Rule for [ticket 11](issues/11-task-update-spec-and-adr.md): **ship every knob on** — shadows, FX, sway, density 1.3 — and **build no automatic downgrade ladder**, because a device-sniffing branch is a permanent cost against a fear no measurement supports. The cut order is written down unused (**shadows → FX → sway → density**, density last because 1.3 is ticket 13's woodland feel, so cutting it is a visual regression); if it is ever built it triggers on a **measured rolling frame time crossing 16.7 ms, never on a user-agent string**, which would have degraded this iPhone for nothing. `prefers-reduced-motion` stays an accessibility rule and must not become the slow-device path.

- [Grilling: the City Theme in 3D](issues/09-grilling-city-theme-3d.md) — ⭐ **the City is not bought. We author its geometry in code, and a storey is an instance.** The first Theme on this map that does not come out of a vendor's zip, and it **redraws the "no making our own 3D models" scope line** — deliberately, on measured evidence, not because the sources failed. Two facts killed the candidates, both from downloaded files rather than vendor pages: **Quaternius Downtown City MegaKit is photoreal PBR** — research 01 ranked it *first* for this Theme without ever seeing it — and **Kenney City Kit (Commercial) costs 1,986 triangles per Entity against the Forest's 150 (13.3×)**, with every building a single primitive on one atlas material, so ticket 06's canopy/trunk split has no equivalent and a risk tint would paint walls, windows and roof together. Kenney's ladder is real, though, and the record should say so: heights step by exactly 0.400 m — one storey — across a 6.1× span, and its 95 road pieces on a 1×1 grid **close the roads-and-terrain gap** the 2D pack left open. Authoring wins because **a building is a box and a tree is not**, and because it **restores the growth mechanic ticket 07 gave up** with Polyfork's `floors` knob: `subscriber_grew` becomes literal, a storey appears. Architecture: author one storey, one roof, one ground floor, one road tile, one street light — **~6 draw calls for the whole City** against the Forest's 41, and three small meshes to build instead of 36 baked variants. Size Tier is **1 / 2 / 4 / 8 storeys**, an 8× span that a viewer reads by *counting floors*, so `hash(id)` must never offset it; footprint never grows, because placement is prefix-stable. **The facade belongs to risk and variety moves up to the roof** — ticket 06's rule that colour must paint the silhouette forced this, and neutral facades make the multiply land identically on every building, exactly as the always-green canopy did. **Dusk**, because it is the only light in which a lit window is information. Flat street grid on a raised slab with a hard edge — the Forest's rolling ground does not port, so roads carry depth instead. Churn wave is guarded by **street lights that never go out** and a construction fence that says *redevelopment, not ruin*. One risk left deliberately open for the gate: rain at dusk over a grey city may read as gloom. Creates [ticket 17](issues/17-prototype-3d-city-gate.md); [ticket 11](issues/11-task-update-spec-and-adr.md) gains a second ADR and now blocks on 17; [ticket 10](issues/10-grilling-rts-theme-3d.md) must be re-read before it runs.

- [Grilling: what happens to the PixiJS prototype](issues/14-grilling-pixijs-fate.md) — **the pixel World is gone; the Signal layer it was built on is not.** No `/demo`, no second Theme, no easter egg (user: *"pixelwereld nee zeker niet"*) — a `/demo` on the pixel renderer would have been the two-render-modes rule wearing a hat. `prototypes/forest-world/` is deleted. **Three files were never 2D and moved to `prototypes/signals/`**: `mock-signals.js` (the generator *and* `foldWorldState`), ticket 12's `test.mjs`, and the pre-fix `test-snapshot.json`. The directory name is the point — `CONTEXT.md` makes **Signal** the layer Themes consume and never bypass, so the tree now says what ADR-0001 says; putting them inside `forest-world-3d/` would have implied the generator belongs to the 3D Theme. Three live references repointed, `node prototypes/signals/test.mjs` green on all four checks, artifact rebuilt and still self-contained. **Git is the record** — nothing archived into an `archive/` folder, that being what a repo without history has to do. **Closed tickets keep their old paths on purpose**: they record a state that was true when written, and only open documents ([ticket 11](issues/11-task-update-spec-and-adr.md) and the spec) were repointed.

- [Prototype: the 3D Forest World — the gate](issues/08-prototype-3d-forest-gate.md) — ⭐ **the gate passed: "it lands 100× better than the pixel world"** (user, 2026-08-24), with *yes* on scrubbing and *nothing lost* from the sprites. **The pivot is committed** — PixiJS 8 and the self-made pixel sprites are retired as the render stack, the map's "no production work until the gate passes" rule is discharged, and every ticket downstream is live. The first look was only **"it's fine"**; three user objections turned it, in an order worth keeping: **rolling ground** (`relief: 0.9`, one draw call) so depth reads while the camera stands still, **density 1.3 not 3.0** because ticket 04's occlusion arithmetic had bought visibility at the cost of the forest, and **a camera the viewer can move, behind a long lens** — the finding that sent the whole camera question back to [ticket 13](issues/13-grilling-camera-redecided.md). Ground and density fixed *the World*; the camera fixed *the reading of it*; neither alone earned the verdict. Certified: **41 draw calls flat** at 237 / 1,583 / 3,167 Entities, ~3.3 ms total CPU per replay frame against a 16.7 ms budget, ticket 05's height ladder reproduced to the millimetre, and ADR-0001 proved by driving the whole 3D Theme off the unchanged 2D mock generator. **The one debt: real frame rates are still uncertified** — no GPU on this machine, so the verdict is on the look alone; the measurement graduates to [ticket 15](issues/15-task-measure-device-fps.md) rather than dying here. Unblocks [tickets 09](issues/09-grilling-city-theme-3d.md) and [10](issues/10-grilling-rts-theme-3d.md); graduates [14](issues/14-grilling-pixijs-fate.md) and [16](issues/16-grilling-entity-animation.md).

- [Grilling: the camera, re-decided](issues/13-grilling-camera-redecided.md) — **a free camera behind a ~20° long lens, with one button that straightens it up.** This is now the map's single camera model; [ticket 04](issues/04-grilling-camera-and-framing.md) is history, not reference. The gate proved a camera the viewer cannot move does not read as 3D, so the guarantee moves from *constraint* to *recovery*: **every frame is reachable — yaw free, pitch free and clamped 10°–78° (the user chose the wide clamp after driving both extremes) — and the four stands survive as a recover control** that steps the yaw 90° *and* eases the pitch back to default. **Orthographic loses to the long lens** (the projection that shows volume is the one the pivot ships), which costs the word *isometric* and costs zoom-at-cursor — zoom goes to frame centre. Two of ticket 04's numbers were void arithmetic and are re-chosen on looks alone: **the opening frame stays 35.264° / zoom 1.9** because it is the frame that earned the gate's "feels more 3D", and **density is 1.3 cells per Entity**, a woodland-feel number now that turning solves occlusion. **The share link keeps its curated night diorama and loses the snap** — a camera that jumps on first touch reads as a bug. Scrub-persist, reload-reset and camera-held-during-replay all survive, the reset now doubling as the second way out of a bad angle. `CONTEXT.md`'s **World** entry is rewritten in-session (no camera detail in a glossary); **[ticket 11](issues/11-task-update-spec-and-adr.md) owes the spec §4 camera section and a new camera ADR** (Q10).

- [Grilling: the Signal vocabulary in 3D](issues/06-grilling-signal-vocabulary-3d.md) — **every Signal fits in two per-Entity channels — an instance matrix and an RGB multiply — plus three fixed-size FX pools. No extra render pass.** The whole vocabulary is designed inside instancing rather than around it, so the World stays ~38 draw calls at any Entity count. Two holes closed: **Kenney ships no dead standing tree**, so recent churn is now *the tree it died at*, its canopy tint ramping green → `#7d6f5f` across the whole 90-day window (truer than 2D's generic snag, free as a colour lerp, and it says *how recently* a Subscriber was lost); and **`returned` was never designed in 2D** — it now gets five cool-white sparks against `appeared`'s three gold, over a stump that visibly becomes a tree, the one row where 3D says what 2D structurally could not. **The canopy and trunk become separate `InstancedMesh`es** (28 Entity meshes, not 15) because `setColorAt` multiplies the whole instance and a single mesh would turn the trunk orange — this restores the exact 2D risk semantics for ~13 extra draw calls. Payment clouds show only when zoomed in (a third of Entities carry one, which from a fixed pitch is a ceiling), while drops, and every colour state, survive at every zoom: **FX are world-space and never compensate for zoom** — colour reads at full zoom-out because it paints the silhouette. Failure-cloud flicker moves from alpha (unavailable) to brightness, and gains the per-Entity phase 2D lacked. `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured and sway is desktop-only, both closing gaps in the prototype. **Ticket 08, the gate, is now unblocked — every prerequisite is resolved.**

- [Grilling: free tier or paid, and which plan](issues/07-grilling-free-vs-pro.md) — **Polyfork spend is €0: no plan, and no free account either.** Ticket 05 zeroed the bake budget and ADR-0003's quantile buckets left nothing for a parametric knob to express, so the free tier's two offerings are both dead and Kenney (CC0, already downloaded, 3–10× lighter) makes its free Forest assets redundant. The last live thread — *"colour-only knobs cannot be pre-baked"* — is cut by [ticket 06](issues/06-grilling-signal-vocabulary-3d.md): every colour is a `setColorAt` multiply in our own renderer, so colour never touches the asset pipeline. Let the Founders Club seats go. **Ticket 03's five shipping conditions all lapse**, and Quaternius being CC0 also retires the CC-BY credits page the 2D RTS decision required. The asset story is one line for ticket 11: **CC0 throughout, no licence email, no vendor, no expiry, no recurring cost.**

- [Grilling: camera and framing](issues/04-grilling-camera-and-framing.md) — ⚠️ **superseded 2026-08-24 by [Grilling: the camera, re-decided](issues/13-grilling-camera-redecided.md)**, which is the camera the app ships. Dead here: the orthographic projection, the four-stand rule, the fixed pitch and its occlusion arithmetic, the 3.0 density, the share link's snap-on-touch, and "isometric" in the glossary. Surviving, restated in ticket 13: free clamped zoom, the 400 ms translate-only `centerOn` with its raycast fallback, camera state across scrub / reload / share link, the camera held during replay, and the Ranger station layout. Kept below as the record of why the constraint looked right before the gate was built. As decided: **orthographic, fixed pitch at true isometric (35.264°), snap-rotating between four diagonal yaw stands, free clamped zoom.** A deliberate middle: the camera moves, but only between four known states — buying a way out of occlusion without buying a look nobody can guarantee. **The pitch changed on arithmetic**: the 2D prototype's 32×16 tile is `atan(0.5)` = 26.565°, at which ticket 05's 2.51-unit tier-4 Entity hides **5.02 tiles** of depth; 35.264° cuts that to 3.55, and the grid opens from 1.9 to 3.0 cells per Entity (~2.3× fewer hidden Entities overall). Both are knobs for [ticket 08](issues/08-prototype-3d-forest-gate.md) to show side by side. Occlusion is explicitly *not* fixed by transparency (fights instancing) or by sorting tall Entities back (forbidden by prefix-stable placement). `centerOn` flies 400 ms, translate-only, rotating only when a raycast says the target is blocked. Camera persists across a scrub, resets on reload. **The share link gets its own hand-tuned night diorama position that snaps to the nearest stand on first touch** — this retires variant C as a separate layout and graduates the map's share-link fog item. Variant B survives intact, plus two yaw buttons. `CONTEXT.md`'s **World** entry updated; "isometric" becomes *more* accurate. **[Ticket 06](issues/06-grilling-signal-vocabulary-3d.md) is now unblocked and is the last thing before the gate.**

- [Grilling: how a Size Tier becomes geometry](issues/05-grilling-size-tier-geometry.md) — **a Size Tier is swapped, never rebuilt; one mechanism for all three Themes; Kenney Nature Kit (CC0) supplies the Forest ladder.** Rebuild exists only at Polyfork, which has zero RTS units and Pro-only City buildings, so it could never be universal — and ADR-0003 makes Size Tier four quantile *buckets*, with no continuum for a knob to express. **Research 01's `small → default → tall` ladder does not exist**: Kenney's family names do not track height (`pineTallA` 1.530 is *shorter* than `pineDefaultA` 1.546). The ladder is selected by measured height instead — 4 tiers × 2–4 variants = **13 living geometries + 2 dead**, spanning 2.27× in mesh height, lifted to ~3.07× by a uniform scale spanning 1.47× (silhouette leads, scale only emphasises). A tier change plays a ~600 ms scale tween live and in replay, and hard-cuts on scrub. Variant comes from `hash(id)` as a [0,1) fraction indexed into the tier's list, so a Subscriber keeps its character across tiers. **Bake budget is now zero and [ticket 07](issues/07-grilling-free-vs-pro.md) is answered "no Polyfork"**; [ticket 03](issues/03-research-polyfork-licence-delivery.md)'s five shipping conditions lapse. Two build notes for [ticket 08](issues/08-prototype-3d-forest-gate.md): all pines share two materials with zero textures, and **`metallicFactor` is 1 kit-wide — a Kenney export quirk that must be overridden to 0** or the forest renders near-black.

- [Task: fix the O(n²) rescan in `foldWorldState`](issues/12-task-fix-fold-performance.md) — **fixed: 201.5 ms → 0.75 ms per fold at 1,583 Entities (269×)**, output byte-identical. `lastMrrBeforeChurn`'s per-churned-Entity rescan is gone; the fold now carries `lastMrr` forward through its single pass and a churned Entity keeps the Size Tier it died at. The curve is linear in Timeline length — ~2.5–6.9 ms at 3,167 Entities, an order of magnitude under a 16.7 ms frame. **Ticket 08 is unblocked on this axis**: a bad scrubbing verdict now means 3D, not a bug. New committed harness `prototypes/forest-world/test.mjs` (`node prototypes/forest-world/test.mjs`) tests determinism, ticket 06's prefix-placement assertion, a per-Entity fold snapshot recorded *before* the fix, and the benchmark. **Rule for [ticket 11](issues/11-task-update-spec-and-adr.md):** production must carry `lastMrr` forward too — a rescan is invisible at demo scale and fatal at customer scale. Benefits PixiJS and three.js equally.

- [Research: three.js in Next.js, at World scale](issues/02-research-threejs-nextjs-scale.md) — **three.js + React Three Fiber, client-only via `next/dynamic({ssr:false})`** — the same mount pattern PixiJS used, and **bundle-neutral** (~232 KB gzip vs PixiJS's ~246 KB). The Entity population is **one `InstancedMesh` per geometry, not per Entity**, so the whole World is **~16–22 draw calls independent of Entity count**; health state is a per-instance `setColorAt` multiply over `COLOR_0`. Measured: 0.400 ms/frame to rewrite all 1,583 instance matrices and colours, 0.577 ms per hover raycast. A whole Theme downloads as **125 KB gzipped, flat in the Entity count** — no Draco. **The real bottleneck is not the renderer**: `foldWorldState` costs 127.8 ms per fold at 1,583 Entities, engine-independent → [ticket 12](issues/12-task-fix-fold-performance.md). Real frame rates are **not certified** — no GPU on the research machine; ticket 08 must measure a laptop and a phone.

- [Research: Polyfork licence and asset delivery in a shipped build](issues/03-research-polyfork-licence-delivery.md) — **safe with conditions**, nothing blocks the gate. The licence names "websites" and "products you sell"; one clause ("no offering them through tools that hand the files to other people") stays ambiguous for a web app, and a ready-to-send email to `hello@polyfork.dev` is drafted. Conditions: send it before public launch; **vendor the GLBs and self-host** (no SLA, no bandwidth cap on their CDN); never ship a `-preview.glb` of a paid asset (they are publicly fetchable at full fidelity); bake with a free-account key and commit the output; expose no files as files. Bakes are a build-time cost only (~72 for three Themes, re-fetch is free), and **colour-only knobs cannot be baked into a GLB at all**.
- [Research: low-poly 3D asset sources](issues/01-research-lowpoly-sources.md) — **no single source carries all three Themes.** Forest → Kenney Nature Kit (CC0, 329 models, a shipped small→default→tall pine ladder, 16–230 tri); City → Quaternius Downtown City MegaKit (CC0, 315); RTS → **Quaternius Ultimate Fantasy RTS** (CC0, 128, "buildings in different evolution stages" + rigged units) — the only source that closes the units gap neither pixel art nor Polyfork could. Polyfork's free tier is props, not Entities. Two corrections: the `.mjs` is free for free assets, and no single `tallness` knob spans four tiers (chain assets *and* knobs). Recommendation: CC0 baseline, Polyfork bought later per Theme only where the parametric knob visibly wins.

## Out of scope

- ~~**Making our own 3D models**~~ — **redrawn 2026-08-24 by [ticket 09](issues/09-grilling-city-theme-3d.md)** and no longer out of scope. The line said "this map buys or licenses assets; authoring is a fresh effort **if every source fails**". No source failed — the City authors its geometry because a building is a box, because it restores Polyfork's `floors` growth mechanic for free, and because the best CC0 kit costs 13.3× the Forest's triangles per Entity. **Authoring is now a live option for any Theme**, decided per Theme on its own merits; it is not a precedent, and the Forest stays on Kenney CC0. Still out of scope: **Blender**, or any authoring tool outside the repo — geometry is generated in code so the pipeline keeps ending at `geometry.json` with no loader and no new tool.
